HEXWAKE v0.1.0

Run Hexwake.exe, choose a starting weapon, and click Begin.
Keep this entire extracted folder together.

WASD: move       Mouse: aim       Space: jump / hop
Shift: mobility  F: auto/manual   Left mouse: manual fire
R: reload        E: collect gun/relic
1-6: select gun  1-3: upgrade choice
Escape: frozen inventory / pause / resume
M: map and settings

Single-player Windows x64 development prototype.
No Unity installation is needed to play.

Source, updates and release notes:
