# Development journal

Write entries as `YYYY-MM-DD-short-title.md`. Keep them useful and honest: what we tried, what happened, what we learned, and what we'll try next. Screenshots or short clips can help once there is something to show.

## Suggested entry shape

- Goal for the session.
- What changed, with links to relevant work.
- What we observed, including failures and surprises.
- Where AI helped and where human judgment or correction mattered.
- The next small step.

The journal is not a requirement for every commit. Write when there is something worth remembering.
