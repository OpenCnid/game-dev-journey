# Choosing a reference: Vampire Hunters

**Date:** 2026-09-30

The project owner has been playing Vampire Hunters and wants to understand its design well enough to create a similar concept. They identified stacking weapons and creating powerful builds as the part they most want to explore.

We reviewed the developer's Steam description and created a [design study](../design/vampire-hunters-study.md). The study separates sourced features, untested interpretations, and questions to investigate through play. We have not measured gameplay or examined the original implementation.

The [proposed first experiment](../../prototypes/001-stacked-weapons/README.md) starts with a minute of basic combat, then tests three simultaneous weapons, and only then adds a short survival loop with contrasting upgrade paths. Its counts, timings, and rules are our proposals. The point is to find out whether the interaction feels good before committing to a large game.

AI helped structure the research questions and break the proposal into implementable systems. Gameplay judgment remains untested: a written specification cannot establish whether the result will be fun.
