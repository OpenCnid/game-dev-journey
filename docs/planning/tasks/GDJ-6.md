The owner initially accepted **cooperative PvE for 2-4 players, testing two first**, with **isekai into an AI-controlled world**, then selected **The Engine Kingdoms**: an AI maintains a magical world and teammates combine forbidden powers. [Decision 0002](../../decisions/0002-cooperative-pve-ai-world.md) and the foundation supersede the earlier single-player constraint. In this design chat the owner selected preventing another catastrophe as the AI's purpose; forbidden combinations threaten the world, but outsiders may need them to save it. The [world comparison](../../design/ai-world-options.md) preserves setting choices; the [encounter record](../../design/engine-kingdoms-first-encounter.md) preserves the later world discussion. Detailed mechanics remain proposals.

**Latest owner clarification, October 3:** [decision 0008](../../decisions/0008-bullet-heaven-solo-play-and-core-experience.md) updates the product to **1-4 player bullet-heaven survival roguelite, solo or cooperative**. Prioritize quick entry, survival, pleasant level-ups and the performant core before surrounding run structure; non-kill progression sources remain possible. Inner energy is a naming recommendation; adding/deepening capabilities remains exploratory. [Decision 0009](../../decisions/0009-weapon-control-and-accessible-combat.md) accepts configurable auto-shoot, varied targeting and through-wall enemy visibility, with secondary/passive automation a possibility. GDJ-11/GDJ-12 hold future position experiments and High-priority readability/accessibility research. These clarify the core without accepting every encounter proposal or implementing gameplay.

**Latest XP/death direction:** [decision 0010](../../decisions/0010-xp-pickups-and-death-participation.md) records enemy-drop XP orbs requiring collection, magnets and percentage pickup-range upgrades. Shared XP is credited on collection. Dead players retain gear-upgrade access through earned choices and should remain involved and return to combat. Future High-priority GDJ-13 researches death/revival; GDJ-14 researches shared legendary edge cases. Exact timers/lives/new-choice eligibility and solo/full-party endings remain open.
