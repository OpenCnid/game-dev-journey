# GDJ-16: readable damage in the rifle arena

## Outcome and reason

Implement [GDJ-16](https://linear.app/cnid/issue/GDJ-16/show-enemy-health-and-enemyplayer-damage-clearly) for the foundation's readable, responsive combat pillar. In the [GDJ-2 baseline](../../playtests/2026-10-03-rifle-baseline.md), the developer could not identify wounded two-hit drones when kiting a group. Preserve that exact candidate as the control.

## Implementation contract, recorded before code changes

- Simulation remains the only writer of health, hits, kills and respawns. Expose read-only enemy health and an immutable shot result containing the hit point, target incarnation, actual damage and health after damage, captured before a killed target respawns. Preserve the existing firing API and cadence for benchmark/reference callers.
- Presentation reads health every frame; it never estimates it from shot count. Show full and wounded bars above on-screen enemies within 40 metres, with two segments for the current two-hit drone. Limit the list to the nearest 64 visible enemies. Do not create an object per enemy. This flat arena has no interior occluders; depth-aware visibility and dense-group decluttering remain future work.
- Use a bounded pool of brief world-space impact sparks (white hit, larger gold kill), a short hit marker and longer gold kill marker. Capture death effects at the old hit point, so they cannot follow a respawned slot. No shader/backend promotion or tracer change is required.
- Replace the existing 0.2-second full-screen red wash with a 0.35-second edge pulse and 0.65-second actual-health-loss label, keeping the aim area readable. Health loss, not contact proximity, triggers it.
- Freeze transient damage cues during pause. Let the last cues expire after a win/loss; clear them on restart, ready state and benchmark entry. Enemy health bars remain inspectable while paused and disappear after the run ends. Disable the new feedback path in synthetic benchmarks to preserve their workload definition.

## Human revision contract, recorded before implementation

P1 recognized first-hit wounds and player damage/death, but found death particles small and rapid-fire white feedback masked by gold. A subsequent screenshot shows clustered mint bars merging into a ribbon. Revise the same readability outcome: independent inner white hit and outer gold kill markers, a larger/longer death burst, a brief muzzle pulse for every shot, and a persistent skewed/squashed wounded body/core pose tied to actual health. The pose is presentation-only; current hit geometry and damage stay unchanged. Apply it in both reference and culled paths, omitting it from benchmark workloads.

For clustered bars, keep up to 64 projected candidates but display at most 12. Prioritize the nearest enemy intersected by the current aim ray, then wounded enemies, then other nearby enemies. Try the original position and small upward offsets, retain a prior offset while it remains free, and draw a short owner connector. Hide lower-priority bars when bounded label space runs out. Never merge different enemies' health into one number. The target query is read-only and shared with firing. Physical crowd separation stays unchanged; bar layout cannot solve body overlap.

Crosshair target coloring and body ×1/head ×2 critical damage are saved in [GDJ-17](https://linear.app/cnid/issue/GDJ-17/highlight-aimed-enemies-and-add-2-critical-head-hits), with chakra-point/death-qi/dantian naming provisional. Future critical regions must remain aligned with any wounded visual pose. Revised clutter/deformation and rapid-fire readability need another human comparison; the first candidate's confirmations are retained as evidence rather than treated as final acceptance.

## Second human revision contract

On intermediate source `35ba4bb3c75fbed811137c28d95160eeb07f8fcc`, P1 liked the wounded shape but still could not assign floating rows of bars to the clustered enemies. Their [crowd screenshot](../../playtests/evidence/gdj-16-damage/human-floating-bars-35ba.png) supports that ownership problem. They asked whether the apparent body split affects hitboxes or requires more complex damage geometry. It is a visual skew/compression of the original body and core, not separate physics pieces; the unchanged sphere is an approximate hit volume. Retain the simple cue for this prototype and require visible-pose alignment before GDJ-17 adds precision critical regions.

Supersede the twelve-label upward-offset strategy: display one prominent bar for the nearest aimed enemy, then small bars attached to nearby wounded cores, capped at six total. Hide healthy background labels and any lower-priority label whose original screen bounds overlap a retained label. Do not move labels into floating rows or aggregate health. The existing wounded silhouette remains a second way to recognize suppressed wounded enemies. Preserve health, physical crowd spacing and the shared read-only target query. Technical QA also caught HUD marker/connector rotation using the wrong pivot under the 1080p GUI scale; compose rotations in logical GUI coordinates and check 1×/1.5×/2× anchors before rebuilding. Final ownership, label switching and clutter still require human comparison.

## Known future interactions

### Third human revision contract

P1 rejected source `34f5244648d4df77090bd3b9d0e59c71697c424e`: the wide target bar still had no clear owner, the scene was really cluttered, and labels flickered despite wounded bars helping somewhat. They warned about higher-health enemies. Replace the floating target label with one fixed HP panel below the crosshair and matching brackets around the projected body/core. Briefly retain the last health result when aim leaves an enemy, marking it as last-hit information; never imply continued target lock or apply damage from UI state. Keep up to three small wounded labels with retained owner/incarnation slots rather than reselecting by distance each frame. Suppress collisions in slot order, and delay reappearance for 0.25 seconds after an overlap to prevent threshold flicker. Respawn/reset must discard stale owners. Preserve the simple wounded cue and current hit geometry. Higher health remains a future enemy rule; presentation must read health rather than rely on two hit animations.

No per-kill health panel or numeric pop-up is needed. Further high-health readability remains a future-content check.

### Fifth human revision contract

P1 disliked the full cyan HP bar in source `25d8157c97c58fe66331c2401c1e4cac58c91a6f` and could not tell whether aiming or shooting triggered it. It appeared from the aim query before damage. Normal enemies must show HP only while actually wounded; keep helpful aimed-body brackets independent of that bar policy. A healthy target must neither draw an HP bar nor reserve space that hides wounded labels. First actual health loss enables the wounded bar, and full-health respawns/reset hide it. Bosses may eventually keep visible HP at full health, using an explicit authoritative enemy/type visibility policy rather than the aim state or the synthetic benchmark's boss setting. No combat boss type is added here. P1 also requests easily imported custom crosshairs inspired by Aim Lab, KovaaK's and Valorant; investigate independently with subagents, preserve findings and scope implementation separately from this visibility correction.

### Sixth human revision contract

On source `586b1f5b9a655ad8bd0684771088b050c55a5897`, P1 now rejects cyan body brackets: their color matches the selected HP border and the brackets obscure HP. They request floating bars clearly above the head, like an MMO status display perceived by players isekai'ed into the physical world. Supersede the earlier bracket retention: remove body brackets and selected cyan HP decoration. Use the same dark border/amber wounded-health treatment for aimed and background bars. Retain the read-only aim query for target HP priority, without drawing a healthy-target decoration. Place bars 0.45 metres above the posed head anchor, with at least 12 logical HUD units between the bottom of the bar and the highest projected head corner, including the simple wounded pose. Keep owner connectors restrained, actual-damage visibility, retained owner/incarnation slots and overlap recovery; do not detach labels into rows or change combat geometry. Boss visibility remains a future policy. Extend the existing projection/layout and runtime checks for actual head clearance; human clutter/ownership acceptance remains necessary.

Shot results carry target incarnation and health outcome rather than encoding a rifle-specific animation. Later weapons can use the same damage-result contract, and future authoritative cooperative events can add source/owner attribution. Health maxima currently belong to the one drone type; new enemy types need per-type/per-entity maxima. The bounded presentation pool may replace older effects under simultaneous weapon bursts; it must never drop authoritative damage. Later through-wall visibility, ranged threats, upgrades and accessibility settings should revise presentation without moving health ownership.

Ammo, crosshair-gated autofire, jump, ranged enemies, traits and rifle tracer alignment are outside this change. GDJ-15 owns the tracer follow-up. A 35-drone check cannot establish performance at 20,000 enemies.
