# Current state

Updated October 3, 2026. This is an orientation page, not a copy of the task board. Update it when the playable capability, accepted configuration or a major constraint changes.

Start a fresh session with the [project foundation](foundation.md), [planning handoff](planning/README.md), then this page and the selected task's relevant context. The foundation defines the game; this page reports how much of it exists. Planning and task sessions write durable outcomes back before ending.

## What exists

The active Unity project is [prototypes/001-stacked-weapons](../prototypes/001-stacked-weapons/README.md). Unity **6000.3.25f1** and its packages are pinned. `game/` is not the active project.

Gameplay is a **local, one-player** one-minute arena with one automatically firing rifle, movement/aim, health, feedback, pause/restart and a run-ending state. Networking, cooperative sessions, teammate interactions and AI-world presentation are not implemented. Three simultaneous weapons, pickups and upgrades are not implemented yet. Initial developer observations and the completed baseline control checks are recorded below; they support a revision rather than broad enjoyment or cooperative acceptance.

**October 3 GDJ-2 baseline:** an exact release player from clean source `28ca1bce4d71d0af82326a93f65a528ebfd1cd5c` is available with the default configuration. Current checks passed 33 Edit Mode and two Play Mode tests, plus standalone firing/rendering and log inspection. The developer confirmed at least two active attempts, pause freezing the countdown and R restoring 100 HP/60 seconds/0 kills, and called the slice pretty solid. They could not read wounded enemy health for group-kiting and reported laggy shot lines/projectile-like feel. The [rifle baseline report](playtests/2026-10-03-rifle-baseline.md) selects revise before stacking: one next damage-readability outcome in [GDJ-16](https://linear.app/cnid/issue/GDJ-16/show-enemy-health-and-enemyplayer-damage-clearly), with aim/tracer alignment separately scoped in [GDJ-15](https://linear.app/cnid/issue/GDJ-15/align-rifle-hitscan-and-tracer-feedback-during-fast-turns). Ammo, target-gated autofire, jumping and ranged threats are saved requests. That baseline report introduced no gameplay change; the GDJ-16 candidate below adds health/damage visibility.

The stress harness supports up to **20,000 synthetic ability-heavy bosses**. Jobs/Burst simulation and culled instancing are defaults. Native state, compact indirect projectiles and combined character rendering remain selectable experiments; a combined development preset has not been promoted.

## What the evidence supports

**GDJ-16 candidate, October 3:** [enemy health and damage feedback](playtests/2026-10-03-damage-readability.md) is implemented in an isolated candidate from clean source `52650ce35aebd3ac43311a547218cd1d317fb200`. Full/wounded health bars, white hit/gold kill sparks and markers, and player damage edge pulses/amounts read authoritative outcomes. All 39 Edit Mode and three Play Mode checks passed and a release build/startup check succeeded. P1 recognized wounded health and player damage/death; small death cues, suppressed rapid-fire hit feedback and stacked bars prompted a revision. The first played build is archived and revised readability still needs comparison. The GDJ-2 control is preserved; no new performance result or renderer promotion accompanies this change.

The historical renderer baseline is `7414fac`. The [September 30 renderer report](playtests/2026-09-30-renderer-experiments.md) records 35 passing automated tests and three full combined-renderer captures at 20,000 synchronized bosses: **400–468 average FPS**, **3.743–4.297 ms p99**, with equal gameplay work and no dropped projectiles/catch-up limit events. These are historical results, not checks rerun by this documentation change.

This establishes a local synthetic percentile result. It does not establish multiplayer synchronization/latency, final-game performance, a hard every-frame minimum, older-hardware support or enjoyment. Warning caching had no isolated win; overlapping preparation had no reliable overall win. Keep optimization work tied to measured costs.

## Direction and constraints

**Baseline-first sequencing correction, October 3:** observe the current rifle before assigning traits or a concrete upgrade pool. [GDJ-2](planning/tasks/GDJ-2.md) already owns the human play observations and proceed/revise decision; use those findings to choose one next revision or progression experiment. The accepted eligibility rule remains saved for later. No new build, play observation or playable capability accompanies this correction.

**Weapon-trait clarification:** [decision 0011](decisions/0011-weapon-traits-and-upgrade-eligibility.md) accepts ordinary enhancement of existing traits and specific compatible items/transformations that add a trait and open future upgrades. Starting rifle traits, exact compatibility and offer/shared-recipient timing remain open. No trait/eligibility/upgrade system is implemented by this documentation checkpoint.

**First-gun/growth clarification:** the owner selected a steady rifle with predictable, frequent shots as the archetype to explore first. Its detailed baseline, upgrades and visual effects remain open; the existing prototype rifle does not establish their acceptance. Chosen effects determine usefulness, and possible late-run chaos should retain useful screen information. The [first-gun discussion](design/engine-kingdoms-first-encounter.md#first-gun-and-readable-growth--discussion-checkpoint) contains selected direction and proposed details. No playable capability changes.

**XP/death clarification, October 3:** [decision 0010](decisions/0010-xp-pickups-and-death-participation.md) adds pickup-required enemy-drop XP orbs, magnets and percentage pickup-range upgrades. Collected XP is shared in cooperative play. Dead players retain gear-upgrade access through earned choices; timed revival and continued participation need prioritized future [GDJ-13](https://linear.app/cnid/issue/GDJ-13/research-death-revival-and-continued-player-participation) research. [GDJ-14](https://linear.app/cnid/issue/GDJ-14/research-shared-legendary-compatibility-and-reward-rules) holds shared-legendary edge cases. All players dead and waiting to respawn ends the run, including solo, despite pending timers. The party restarts through a Retry button. Timers/lives/eligibility, event ordering and restart coordination/reset remain open. No pickup, magnet, progression or multiplayer revival capability is implemented here.

**Core-experience clarification, October 3:** [decision 0008](decisions/0008-bullet-heaven-solo-play-and-core-experience.md) records quick one-gun entry, survival and pleasant level-ups, with future non-kill progression possible. Surrounding run structure, detailed weapon-growth taxonomy and martial-energy naming remain open; inner energy is a recommendation. The owner prioritizes the performant core and broad hardware reach inspired by League of Legends/Valorant. No new benchmark, low-end or multiplayer evidence accompanies this design change.

**MVP/alpha scope clarification, October 3:** the permanent matrix and its proposed preparation/power systems, plus optional third-person/dual-camera support, are parked as extremely low-priority ideas beyond MVP/alpha. The initial cooperative run keeps the first-person direction; it does not require a full meta system. Intended persistent discoveries remain a future direction. [Decision 0007](decisions/0007-roguelike-run-identity-and-camera-exploration.md) preserves the owner's priority choice; no playable capability changes.

The accepted product is a **bullet-heaven survival roguelite for 1-4 players, solo or cooperative PvE, testing two-player cooperation first**, with The Engine Kingdoms as background. Immediate combat starts with one gun and grows through shared XP and independent personal random choices/pool modifiers; draws may overlap between teammates. Selecting a qualifying high-rarity upgrade grants it to everyone and credits the original player; appearing in an offer alone does not grant it. Upgrades must accommodate future weapon/loot/draw effects; [decision 0003](decisions/0003-shared-xp-personal-upgrade-draws.md) and [progression design](design/progression-and-upgrade-pools.md) record these commitments. **Party thinking space is accepted for later work** in [decision 0004](decisions/0004-party-thinking-space.md): planned breaks plus earlier invocation, unlimited mental choices during physical freeze, and readiness-based resume. The milestone and [GDJ-7](https://linear.app/cnid/issue/GDJ-7/build-party-thinking-space-for-cooperative-upgrades) track implementation. None of these systems is implemented yet. Qualifying rare tiers/applicability, detailed cadence/loadout/draw behavior and lifecycle remain open in the [encounter record](design/engine-kingdoms-first-encounter.md) and [brief](planning/tasks/GDJ-6.md). Further lore and special combinations are deferred; previous world choices remain in [decision 0002](decisions/0002-cooperative-pve-ai-world.md). Minimal two-player combat/progression feasibility precedes broader expansion. Preserve existing local evidence; live priority/status belong in [Linear](https://linear.app/cnid/project/first-weapon-stacking-playtest-89b5952f722e). See [workflow](workflow.md), [roadmap](roadmap.md) and [game brief](design/game-brief.md).

The development benchmark machine is **i7-10700K / RTX 3090 / approximately 64 GB RAM**, using the existing 1080p protocol. The goal remains 144 FPS or higher in the eventual supported encounter. GTX 1080 Ti/older-CPU testing is deferred until the game is more polished. [Performance policy](performance.md) and [profiling](profiling.md) define the checks.

[Decision 0009](decisions/0009-weapon-control-and-accessible-combat.md) adds configurable auto-shoot, varied targeting and through-wall enemy visibility direction. [GDJ-11](https://linear.app/cnid/issue/GDJ-11/experiment-with-weapon-positioning-and-arsenal-visibility) holds future position experiments; [GDJ-12](https://linear.app/cnid/issue/GDJ-12/research-combat-readability-and-accessibility) holds highly valued deep research. Existing automatic rifle fire is not an implemented configurable setting; new targeting, outlines and access options are absent. No current capability or evidence changes.

Foundation revision 17 retains [decisions 0005](decisions/0005-technology-magic-and-cultivation.md)/[0006](decisions/0006-capacity-mastery-and-prepared-power.md): advanced AI/technology, mage magic and murim/dantian cultivation in one gun, with usable baseline prepared power, capacity expanded through earned upgrades and personal mastery that strengthens/specializes expression. [GDJ-8](https://linear.app/cnid/issue/GDJ-8/develop-hybrid-gun-capacity-mastery-and-prepared-power-rules) holds later detailed design. Costs, progression mappings and compatibility remain open. No technological, magic or cultivation gameplay system is implemented by this design checkpoint.

[Decision 0007](decisions/0007-roguelike-run-identity-and-camera-exploration.md) adds roguelike/roguelite identity, Risk of Rain/Megabonk references and accepted persistent weapon/upgrade discoveries. None of that persistence is implemented. The local prototype remains first person. Third-person support/switching, exact unlock/reset/ownership rules and permanent power remain open. The [permanent matrix](design/permanent-progression-matrix.md) is an exploration of the owner's tower-defense/Path of Exile inspiration.
