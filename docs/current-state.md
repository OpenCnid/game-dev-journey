## Direction and constraints

**Recovery direction, October 4:** [decision 0012](decisions/0012-disconnect-recovery-and-preserved-return.md) records the owner's five-minute total automatic disconnect-freeze allowance per run and preserved late return. The [research/design draft](design/cooperative-session-recovery.md) proposes withdrawal/readiness/reward rules and separates live-host reconnection from checkpoint-backed host recovery. These features are absent from the current fixed prototype, which still aborts on disconnect. Ordinary upgrade-thinking time remains unlimited; no service/package choice or new runtime evidence.

**Baseline-first sequencing correction, October 3:** observe the current rifle before assigning traits or a concrete upgrade pool. [GDJ-2](planning/tasks/GDJ-2.md) already owns the human play observations and proceed/revise decision; use those findings to choose one next revision or progression experiment. The accepted eligibility rule remains saved for later. No new build, play observation or playable capability accompanies this correction.

**Weapon-trait clarification:** [decision 0011](decisions/0011-weapon-traits-and-upgrade-eligibility.md) accepts ordinary enhancement of existing traits and specific compatible items/transformations that add a trait and open future upgrades. Starting rifle traits, exact compatibility and offer/shared-recipient timing remain open. No trait/eligibility/upgrade system is implemented by this documentation checkpoint.

**First-gun/growth clarification:** the owner selected a steady rifle with predictable, frequent shots as the archetype to explore first. Its detailed baseline, upgrades and visual effects remain open; the existing prototype rifle does not establish their acceptance. Chosen effects determine usefulness, and possible late-run chaos should retain useful screen information. The [first-gun discussion](design/engine-kingdoms-first-encounter.md#first-gun-and-readable-growth--discussion-checkpoint) contains selected direction and proposed details. No playable capability changes.

**XP/death clarification, October 3:** [decision 0010](decisions/0010-xp-pickups-and-death-participation.md) adds pickup-required enemy-drop XP orbs, magnets and percentage pickup-range upgrades. Collected XP is shared in cooperative play. Dead players retain gear-upgrade access through earned choices; timed revival and continued participation need prioritized future [GDJ-13](https://linear.app/cnid/issue/GDJ-13/research-death-revival-and-continued-player-participation) research. [GDJ-14](https://linear.app/cnid/issue/GDJ-14/research-shared-legendary-compatibility-and-reward-rules) holds shared-legendary edge cases. All players dead and waiting to respawn ends the run, including solo, despite pending timers. The party restarts through a Retry button. Timers/lives/eligibility, event ordering and restart coordination/reset remain open. No pickup, magnet, progression or multiplayer revival capability is implemented here.

**Core-experience clarification, October 3:** [decision 0008](decisions/0008-bullet-heaven-solo-play-and-core-experience.md) records quick one-gun entry, survival and pleasant level-ups, with future non-kill progression possible. Surrounding run structure, detailed weapon-growth taxonomy and martial-energy naming remain open; inner energy is a recommendation. The owner prioritizes the performant core and broad hardware reach inspired by League of Legends/Valorant. No new benchmark, low-end or multiplayer evidence accompanies this design change.

**MVP/alpha scope clarification, October 3:** the permanent matrix and its proposed preparation/power systems, plus optional third-person/dual-camera support, are parked as extremely low-priority ideas beyond MVP/alpha. The initial cooperative run keeps the first-person direction; it does not require a full meta system. Intended persistent discoveries remain a future direction. [Decision 0007](decisions/0007-roguelike-run-identity-and-camera-exploration.md) preserves the owner's priority choice; no playable capability changes.

The accepted product is a **bullet-heaven survival roguelite for 1-4 players, solo or cooperative PvE, testing two-player cooperation first**, with The Engine Kingdoms as background. Immediate combat starts with one gun and grows through shared XP and independent personal random choices/pool modifiers; draws may overlap between teammates. Selecting a qualifying high-rarity upgrade grants it to everyone and credits the original player; appearing in an offer alone does not grant it. Upgrades must accommodate future weapon/loot/draw effects; [decision 0003](decisions/0003-shared-xp-personal-upgrade-draws.md) and [progression design](design/progression-and-upgrade-pools.md) record these commitments. **Party thinking space is accepted for later work** in [decision 0004](decisions/0004-party-thinking-space.md): planned breaks plus earlier invocation, unlimited mental choices during physical freeze, and readiness-based resume. The milestone and [GDJ-7](https://linear.app/cnid/issue/GDJ-7/build-party-thinking-space-for-cooperative-upgrades) track implementation. None of these systems is implemented yet. Qualifying rare tiers/applicability, detailed cadence/loadout/draw behavior and lifecycle remain open in the [encounter record](design/engine-kingdoms-first-encounter.md) and [brief](planning/tasks/GDJ-6.md). Further lore and special combinations are deferred; previous world choices remain in [decision 0002](decisions/0002-cooperative-pve-ai-world.md). Minimal two-player combat/progression feasibility precedes broader expansion. Preserve existing local evidence; live priority/status belong in [Linear](https://linear.app/cnid/project/first-weapon-stacking-playtest-89b5952f722e). See [workflow](workflow.md), [roadmap](roadmap.md) and [game brief](design/game-brief.md).

The development benchmark machine is **i7-10700K / RTX 3090 / approximately 64 GB RAM**, using the existing 1080p protocol. The goal remains 144 FPS or higher in the eventual supported encounter. GTX 1080 Ti/older-CPU testing is deferred until the game is more polished. [Performance policy](performance.md) and [profiling](profiling.md) define the checks.

[Decision 0009](decisions/0009-weapon-control-and-accessible-combat.md) adds configurable auto-shoot, varied targeting and through-wall enemy visibility direction.
