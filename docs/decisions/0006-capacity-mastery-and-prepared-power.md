# 0006: Capacity mastery and prepared power for hybrid guns

- **Date:** 2026-10-03
- **Status:** accepted design direction; detailed specification and implementation deferred.

## Context

After accepting technology, mage magic and cultivated qi together in [decision 0005](0005-technology-magic-and-cultivation.md), the owner approved the proposed capacity, mastery and prepared-power framework and requested that it be recorded in Linear for further development later.

## Decision and reasoning

Accept three complementary parts of weapon growth:

- **Prepared power:** a gun provides usable baseline capability through its construction and prepared effects, including authored magic or qi. A player can use that baseline while developing personal mastery.
- **Capacity:** the current weapon/build has limits on what power it can sustain. Earned upgrades can expand those limits, supporting growth from a basic gun into a much stronger build.
- **Mastery:** personal understanding and control strengthen or specialize how the player expresses the weapon's power. Mage and cultivation mastery can distinguish builds even when team XP is shared.

This gives broad world possibilities a progression framework while preserving immediate one-gun entry and the large simultaneous-stack fantasy. It accepts baseline prepared power plus personal mastery rather than requiring the operator to personally supply every magical or qi effect. Exact formulas and content remain to be authored.

## Consequences and later work

Further design is recorded in [GDJ-8: Develop hybrid gun capacity, mastery and prepared-power rules](https://linear.app/cnid/issue/GDJ-8/develop-hybrid-gun-capacity-mastery-and-prepared-power-rules). It is later Backlog design work, with no date or immediate implementation requested.

Specify the relationship between shared XP, personal choices, weapon capacity and mastery through a small hybrid-weapon example. Resource costs/bars, capacity measurements, mastery levels, cultivation realms, classes and numerical ceilings remain open. Compatibility, instability and AI enforcement are separate proposals; this acceptance does not select their consequences.

Preserve future weapon/player/loot/draw effects and 1-4 player builds under [decision 0008](0008-bullet-heaven-solo-play-and-core-experience.md). Qi/murim remain internal lore references; the approachable player-facing energy name is not selected yet. Shared rare rewards still need applicability rules when recipients lack matching training, power sources or weapons. The Zone's proposed mental-rehearsal theme can present earned changes; its unlimited decision time does not supply free XP or upgrades.

The first encounter can use a small authored subset. Detailed lore development is not an additional gate requiring a full technology tree, cultivation system or weapon roster. [GDJ-6](https://linear.app/cnid/issue/GDJ-6/design-the-first-engine-kingdoms-cooperative-encounter) still needs qualifying-rare/applicability and lifecycle rules before encounter evaluation.

## Revisit when

Capacity limits obstruct noticeable growth, mastery makes shared rewards unusable, or prepared power erases meaningful build differences. Later play evidence must assess readability, balance and enjoyment; accepting this framework establishes none of those results.
