# 0005: Technology magic and cultivation in weapon builds

- **Date:** 2026-10-03
- **Status:** accepted world and weapon direction; detailed lore rules and implementation pending.

## Context

The owner redirected the next GDJ-6 discussion toward gun lore and what weapons can or cannot do. They established that The Engine Kingdoms supports advanced AI and unrestricted categories of fictional technology, fantasy magic whose possibilities depend on a mage's level, and murim martial artists with dantian cultivation. The owner explicitly confirmed that combining technology, mage magic and cultivated qi in a single gun is central to weapon builds.

## Decision and reasoning

Retain the physical, AI-maintained Engine Kingdoms premise from [decision 0002](0002-cooperative-pve-ai-world.md), with technology, magic and martial cultivation coexisting. The exact AI label or taxonomy is not the current design question. Technological possibilities are deliberately broad in this fiction; this is creative direction, not a real-world claim about what AI can accomplish.

Accept that **a single gun can combine all three traditions, and mixing them is central to builds**. This selects no class system or exclusive pool policy, and does not require every weapon to contain all three from the start or every player to have identical mastery. The one-gun start, earned choices, shared XP, personal draws, selected shared rares and future thinking-space feature remain accepted.

The owner wants rules for guns and their growth, rather than a prerequisite history of the world. The [gun-lore discussion](../design/engine-kingdoms-first-encounter.md#gun-lore-technology-magic-and-cultivation) proposes a common weapon role and limits to evaluate. The distinction between what exists in the world and what a current build can wield is still a proposal; no capacity formula, resource cost, cultivation realm or final weapon roster is selected.

## Consequences and open details

- Spell capability depends on mage level as directed by the owner. Define whether a gun's own prepared spell/core supplies baseline capability or its user must personally supply every spell; the proposed baseline-plus-mastery model is unselected.
- Martial cultivation and the dantian belong to the world direction. Exact qi rules, cultivation stages, techniques and relationships to technology/magic remain to define. Do not import an entire external setting or assume one universal cultivation canon.
- Keep weapon behavior distinct from its source of power. A technological, magical or cultivated contribution can change output, control or another declared effect; none must be a fixed damage type or only a projectile modifier.
- Preserve future weapon/player/loot/draw effects and 2-4 player builds. Shared rares still need applicability rules if a recipient lacks the particular training, source or weapon a proposed effect requires. This decision does not settle that prior open question.
- Gun lore is the current authorized discussion. Extensive history, named factions, a full rank ladder, scripted arrival and special-combination production remain deferred; a full cultivation game or technology tree is not required for the first bounded slice.

## Revisit when

An authored weapon cannot express its contribution clearly, hybrid rules erase distinct builds, tier/cost rules obstruct the large-stack growth fantasy, or shared rewards leave teammates with unusable effects. Use later playable evidence to tune the rules; this design establishes neither balance nor enjoyment.
