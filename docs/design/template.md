# Design: topic

- **Design status:** open / proposed / accepted in part / accepted / superseded. Label mixed sections individually.
- **Scope:** MVP / later / undecided.
- **Owner decision basis:** dated decision links or explicit current instruction; proposed content stays labeled.
- **Upstream:** foundation section, decisions, existing designs or evidence that constrain this topic. Register their IDs in `docs/registry.json`.
- **Implementation evidence:** source/task/report links, or explicitly unimplemented/unverified. Acceptance and implementation are separate.

## Player experience and purpose

What does the player do, perceive or feel? Which pillar and run moment does this serve?

## Rules and examples

Describe chosen behavior and one concrete player example. Keep tuning hypotheses separate from product commitments. Record alternatives only where they help decide an open question.

## Interactions and boundaries

Identify relevant weapons, progression, enemies, player state, co-op, pause and reset interactions. State ownership/known extension needs at the level necessary to plan a bounded implementation; link technical design for engineering detail.

## Open decisions

State the unresolved question, viable options and practical tradeoffs. Identify whether it blocks the next playable slice or can remain open. Never fill a missing decision merely to make the page look complete.

## Downstream plan and evidence

Link task(s), exact source/build and dated evidence. Record human observations separately from automated checks. After a changed decision, update affected documents/trace edges and mark superseded sections.
